Hours per topic

Each module's syllabus hours are shared between its topics by how much content each topic has. The result is adjusted by a difficulty score, then blended with the Linear Regression (ML) estimate (60% syllabus, 40% ML). Lab work counts at 60%, because part of the practice happens in college.

Fitting the deadline

Sessions last 30–90 minutes, with 15-minute breaks, and are placed only inside your study windows. If the full time doesn't fit, every topic is shortened by the same percentage (never below 50%), so every topic still gets time before the deadline.

Preferred time of day

A Decision Tree (ML) suggests Morning, Afternoon, Evening or Night for each topic. This is only a preference and never overrides your availability. Subjects rotate, so you don't study one subject all day.
